Mmodemmo SBA Tracking System

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Mmodemmo SBA Tracking System ("Mmodemmo", "the application", "we") collects, uses, stores, protects, and handles information when a school uses the desktop application and the connected online portal.

Mmodemmo is a school administration tool used by a school's own administrators and teachers to manage that school's attendance, levy, exam-mark, and report-card records. It is not a consumer social or communications product.

1. Information we collect

School records entered into the application — student names and class information, attendance records, levy/fee payment records, SBA and exam marks, and related academic data that the school's administrators and teachers enter directly into the desktop application or online portal.

Teacher access information — the name and contact details a school administrator adds for each teacher, and the PIN used to access the online portal. This is set and controlled by the school administrator, not collected from teachers directly by us.

Google account information (school administrator only) — when a school administrator connects their Google account, the application receives only what is needed to identify the connected account and to read and write data in the Google Sheets and Drive files the application itself creates or is given access to. See Section 3 for the specific Google permissions requested.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the core functions of the application: attendance, levy collection, SBA/mock exam marks, and automatic report card generation.
  • To sync a school's records to that school's own Google Sheet, and to keep an encrypted backup copy in that school's own Google Drive, so records survive a lost, stolen, or damaged computer.
  • To manage teacher access to the online portal as configured by the school administrator.
  • To send SMS notifications the school administrator or teacher chooses to send (e.g. results or notices to parents and staff).
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the application, and to provide support.

We do not use school or student data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Google user data

Mmodemmo requests the following Google OAuth scopes, and only these:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file

Why we request this access. A school administrator connects their school's own Google account so the desktop application can keep the school's records in a Google Sheet the school owns, and store a backup copy of the school database in the school's own Google Drive.

What this access does and does not allow.

  • Google Sheets (spreadsheets scope) — lets the application read and write the school's own spreadsheet data used to sync attendance, levy, and marks records.
  • Google Drive, app-created files only (drive.file scope) — lets the application create and access a backup file inside a folder it creates in the school's Drive. This scope does not give the application access to other files already in the school's Drive that Mmodemmo did not create or that were not explicitly opened through the application.

Whether this data is stored. Yes — the synced Sheet and the backup file are stored in the school's own Google account, not on Mmodemmo's servers. The desktop application also keeps a local copy of the school's database on the school's own computer.

Whether it is shared with third parties or used for advertising. No. Google account data accessed through these scopes is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not sold.

Mmodemmo's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal or school data. We do not share it with other schools. Information is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • The school's own Google account — as described above, records are synced to and backed up in the school's own Google Sheets/Drive, which the school controls.
  • SMS delivery — when a school chooses to send SMS results or notices, the relevant message content (e.g. a phone number and result text) is passed to our SMS delivery provider solely to send that message.
  • Legal requirements — if required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request.

5. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including encrypting the backup copy stored in a school's Google Drive. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

School records are retained on the school's own computer and in the school's own Google account for as long as the school continues to use the application, or for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to any applicable legal requirements. A school can delete its own synced Sheet or backup file directly from its Google account at any time.

7. User rights

A school administrator or teacher may:

  • Ask what information the application holds about their school or account.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information entered into the application.
  • Request deletion of information where applicable.
  • Ask questions about how information is processed.
  • Disconnect the application's access to their Google account at any time.

To exercise any of these, contact cosmopolitech.dev@gmail.com. We will respond to reasonable requests within a practical timeframe.

8. Managing or revoking Google account access

A school administrator can review and revoke Mmodemmo's access to their Google account at any time from their Google Account permissions page. Revoking access stops future syncing and backup to Google; it does not automatically delete records already stored locally on the school's computer or already saved in the school's Google Sheet or Drive backup file, since those remain under the school's own control.

9. Children and students

Mmodemmo is intended for use by school administrators and teachers, not directly by students or parents. Student-related information (such as names, marks, and attendance) is entered by school staff as part of normal school administration, not collected directly from students by Mmodemmo. Schools are responsible for ensuring their use of the application complies with applicable requirements regarding student records.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the application's practices change. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
