Mmodemmo SBA Tracking System

Privacy Policy

Last updated: [today's date]

Mmodemmo SBA Tracking System requests access to two things in a school's own Google account, only after the school signs in and approves it:

  • Google Sheets — to read and write student, teacher, attendance, and fee records that belong to that school.
  • Google Drive (app-created files only) — to store an encrypted backup copy of the school's own database, in a folder the app creates inside the school's own Drive.

All data accessed belongs to the school and stays inside the school's own Google account at all times. It is not shared with, sold to, or made visible to any other school or third party.

A school can revoke access at any time from its own Google Account permissions page.
