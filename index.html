Desktop application · Online teacher & admin portal

Student records, attendance, levies, and report cards — in one connected system.

Mmodemmo SBA Tracking System is a desktop application that helps schools manage student records, attendance, daily fee (levy) collection, and exam marks, with a connected online portal that lets teachers and admins work from any browser.

How your school's data is handled

The application syncs data to a Google Sheet that the school owns and controls, and keeps an encrypted backup copy in the school's own Google Drive. Nothing is shared with, or visible to, any other school or third party. See the full Privacy Policy for details on the exact permissions requested.

What it does

Attendance & levies

Recorded on desktop or through the online portal, by admin or assigned teachers.

SBA & mock exam marks

Entered once, feeding directly into automatic report cards.

Report cards

Terminal and mock exam report cards generated automatically as marks come in.

BECE results analysis

Import BECE result files and get chart-based analysis, exportable to Excel.

SMS notifications

Results sent to parents, and custom notices to staff and parents.

Automatic backup

The school database is backed up into the school's own Google account.
