Desktop application · Online teacher & admin portal

Student records, attendance, levies, and report cards — in one connected system.

Mmodemmo SBA Tracking System is a desktop application that helps schools manage school based assessment (class marks, terminal and mock exams marks), student records, student and teachers attendance, Levy/fee collection, send sms of students performance records to parents and custom sms meetings to parents , with a connected online portal that lets teachers and admins work from any browser

How your school's data is handled

The application syncs data to a Google Sheet that the school owns and controls, and keeps an encrypted backup copy in the school's own Google Drive. Nothing is shared with, or visible to, any other school or third party. See the full Privacy Policy for details on the exact permissions requested.

What it does

SBA & Mock Exam Marks

Entered once, feeding directly into automatic report cards.

Report cards

Terminal and Mock exam report cards generated automatically as marks come in.

Attendance & Levies

Recorded on desktop or through the online portal, by admin or assigned teachers.

SMS Notifications

Results sent to parents, and custom notices to staff and parents.

BECE Results Analysis

Import BECE result files and get chart-based analysis, exportable to Excel.

Automatic Backup

The school database is backed up into the school's own Google account.

Who it's for
School administrators

Access is controlled entirely by the school. The administrator sets up the desktop application, then issues each teacher a PIN and portal link for the specific classes and duties (attendance, levies, or marks) they are assigned to. Access can be reassigned or revoked by the admin at any time.

Security & privacy

Authentication & access control

The school administrator authenticates with their school's Google account so the desktop application can sync data to that school's own Google Sheet and store a backup copy in that school's own Google Drive. Teacher access to the online portal is separately controlled through admin-issued PINs, not through Google sign-in.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. We do not claim the system is unhackable or immune to all risk — no system is.

Google account access

Mmodemmo requests only the Google permissions needed to sync and back up a school's own records:

  • Google Sheets — to read and write the school's own student, teacher, attendance, and fee records.
  • Google Drive (app-created files only) — to store an encrypted backup of the school's database in a folder the app creates inside that school's own Drive. Mmodemmo cannot see or access the rest of a school's Drive.

Mmodemmo does not request access to Gmail, Calendar, Contacts, or any other Google service, and does not use Google account data for advertising.
